    mipilin is a social mood tracker.

    Developed over the course of about half a year, mipilin is designed as a spiritual successor and love letter to imood and other social websites of its era.

    It's currently in a closed beta, and development is on soft hiatus. The intent is to ultimately turn it into a worthy successor to imood that will bring people together and help them understand their feelings in a more personal and less intimidating manner. After years of regularly pseudo-journaling on imood, I can tell you that once it becomes a habit for you to update your mood it appears to become much easier to find ways to express how you're feeling because you're always expressing your emotions in a way that requires you to find a single word to adequately describe it. It doesn't always have to be a commitment, though. The point of it is it's supposed to be fun and potentially beneficial in the long term.

    It is currently hosted on Hack Club servers, but this will change once a more reliable hosting method becomes feasible.
