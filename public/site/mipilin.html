  • mipilin

    mipilin was a social mood tracker.

    Developed over the course of about half a year, mipilin was designed as a spiritual successor and love letter to imood and other social websites of its era.

    The intent was to ultimately turn it into a worthy successor to imood that would have brought people together and help them understand their feelings in a more personal and less intimidating manner. After years of regularly pseudo-journaling on imood, I can tell you that once it becomes a habit to go on there, it becomes much easier to find ways to express how you're feeling because you're always expressing your emotions in a way that requires you to find a single word to adequately describe it. It doesn't always have to be a commitment, though. The point of it is that it's supposed to be fun and potentially beneficial in the long term.

    Many special thanks to the Republic of India for existing so that the .in TLD may be used in one of the greatest domain hacks of all mankind.
