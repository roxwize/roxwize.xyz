# mipilin was a social mood tracker.

Developed over the course of about half a year, [mipilin](https://mipil.in/) was designed as a spiritual successor and love letter to [imood](https://imood.com/) and other social websites of its era.

<!--It's currently in a closed beta, and development is on soft hiatus. -->The intent was to ultimately turn it into a worthy successor to imood that would have brought people together and help them understand their feelings in a more personal and less intimidating manner. After years of regularly pseudo-journaling on imood, I can tell you that once it becomes a habit to go on there, it becomes much easier to find ways to express how you're feeling because you're always expressing your emotions in a way that requires you to find a single word to adequately describe it. It doesn't always have to be a commitment, though. The point of it is that it's supposed to be fun and potentially beneficial in the long term.
